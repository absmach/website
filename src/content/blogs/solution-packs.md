Magistrala cloud platform now ships with ready-to-install solutions, so you can go from a blank domain to a working IoT setup in just a few steps.

We have been hinting at this feature for a while, most recently in our [Magistrala as a Framework](https://absmach.eu/blog/magistrala-as-framework) post, and today we are making it official. Solution packs fundamentally change how you get started with Magistrala. Instead of spending weeks reading documentation and learning the platform before you can build anything, you can now install a working solution in minutes and start sending messages immediately. The platform knowledge can come later, at your own pace.

## What Are Solution Packs?

Solution packs are pre-built, ready-to-install solutions available on the Magistrala platform. Each pack provisions everything a solution needs (devices, channels, data processing rules, dashboards, and more) with a single action. All you need to get started is an account and a domain.

The goal is simple: get you from sign-up to a running solution as fast as possible, without requiring deep knowledge of how Magistrala works under the hood.

## What's Included in a Solution Pack

Each solution pack ships as a complete, working unit. Here is what you get when you install one:

- **Clients**: pre-configured devices or applications, ready to publish messages
- **Channels**: clients are automatically connected to the appropriate channels
- **Groups**: clients and channels are organized into logical groups that mirror real-world separations, such as sites, floors, or zones
- **Rules**: pre-built processing logic that handles your raw messages out of the box. Rules can save data internally or to an external database, trigger alarms, send email or Slack notifications, and forward processed messages to other channels
- **Report configurations**: scheduled or on-demand reports are set up and ready to run
- **Dashboards**: real-time and historical visualizations provisioned and wired to your data sources

Beyond the entities themselves, every solution pack includes two supporting pages:

- **Details page**: an overview of everything the pack ships with: the number of entities, what each is used for, the dashboards included, the reports generated, and more
- **Instructions page**: visible once the solution is installed, this walks you through how to send messages, which configurations to complete, how to share dashboards with your team, and anything else you need to hit the ground running

## How to Get Started

Getting up and running with a solution pack takes just a few steps:

1. Create an account on [Magistrala Cloud](https://cloud.magistrala.absmach.eu)
2. Create a domain
3. Browse the available solution packs and pick one that fits your use case
4. Install the solution pack
5. Follow the instructions page to connect your devices and start sending messages

That's it. No configuration from scratch, no manual wiring of entities. Just a working solution, ready for your data.
