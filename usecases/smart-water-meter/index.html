Smart Metering

Intelligent leak detection and fraud prevention using continuous IoT telemetry to reduce non-revenue water.

View Live Demo

Overview

Water utilities lose a significant portion of produced water due to physical leaks and meter tampering — commonly referred to as Non-Revenue Water (NRW). Traditional billing cycles often reveal these issues weeks too late.

This smart metering solution continuously monitors water consumption telemetry in real time. By analyzing flow rates, volume indices, and security signals, the system detects anomalies immediately, enabling rapid intervention, reduced losses, and improved operational efficiency.

Solution Structure

Smart Meters & Valves

Water meters and control valves deployed across residential sectors, continuously reporting flow and volume data.

IoT Connectivity

Secure transmission of telemetry data via MQTT, CoAP, or HTTP through gateways and edge devices.

Core IoT Platform

Handles device management, access control, real-time message processing, and rule-based automation.

Dashboards & Alerts

Unified dashboards provide visibility into consumption, alarms, and system health across districts and sectors.

Automation & Rules

The platform’s rule engine continuously evaluates incoming telemetry to detect abnormal patterns and security incidents.

  • Automatic leak detection based on consumption thresholds
  • Instant alarms and Slack notifications for maintenance teams
  • Long-term storage of events for audit and compliance

Reports & Insights

Total Consumption Report

Compares billed consumption against supplied water to identify losses at sector and district levels.

Leak Performance Report

Measures the effectiveness of detection rules and highlights areas with persistent leaks.

System Integrity & Fraud Report

Tracks tampering incidents and security violations across the domain.

Benefits

Reduced Water Loss

Detect leaks early and minimize non-revenue water.

Fraud Prevention

Identify meter tampering and unauthorized consumption.

Operational Visibility

Real-time dashboards and alerts improve decision-making.
