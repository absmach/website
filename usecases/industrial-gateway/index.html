Intelligent Cold Storage Monitoring & Compliance

View Live Demo Talk to an IoT Expert

Overview

Magistrala powers IoT deployments where data accuracy, uptime, and traceability are non-negotiable. In critical environments like cold storage and pharmaceutical supply chains, we enable operators to detect issues early, maintain compliance, and integrate monitoring data into existing operational systems without the burden of vendor lock-in.

The reality of cold storage is defined by the high cost of the undetected. A single temperature excursion can destroy high-value inventory, trigger regulatory non-compliance, and cause significant financial loss across food and agricultural supply chains. Manual data collection and delayed response times often leave facilities vulnerable, as fragmented systems across multiple sites make it difficult to prove compliance during essential audits.

Magistrala resolves these vulnerabilities by securely connecting temperature, humidity, and power sensors via standard protocols. By processing telemetry in real-time at the edge or in the cloud, the platform's rules engine identifies anomalies the moment they occur—triggering immediate alerts via email or SMS before spoilage takes hold. This creates a continuous, secure record of environmental status that is instantly available for audit readiness and seamless integration with existing ERP or warehouse management systems.

Built for long-running, production-grade infrastructure, Magistrala is designed for total architectural neutrality. Our open architecture ensures interoperability with existing hardware, allowing operators to scale city-wide or global deployments confidently while maintaining long-term flexibility through open standards.

Key Challenges Addressed

  • Undetected temperature fluctuations leading to spoilage
  • Manual data collection and delayed response times
  • Difficulty proving compliance during audits
  • Fragmented systems across multiple storage sites

Solution Structure

Using Magistrala, temperature, humidity, and power sensors are securely connected to the platform via standard protocols. Data is processed in real time at the edge or in the cloud, with rules configured to trigger alerts when thresholds are exceeded.

Collected telemetry is stored securely and made available through dashboards and APIs, allowing integration with existing warehouse management, ERP, or compliance reporting systems.
