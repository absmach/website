Protecting High-Value Inventory with Real-Time IoT

View Live Demo Talk to an IoT Expert

Overview

Prevent spoilage, ensure compliance, and reduce operational risk with real-time temperature and humidity monitoring powered by Abstract Machines’ IoT platform.

Cold storage failures are expensive. A single temperature excursion can destroy high-value inventory, trigger regulatory violations, and damage customer trust. Manual inspections and delayed reporting leave operators blind to real-time risk.

Abstract Machines enables continuous monitoring of temperature and humidity across cold rooms, warehouses, and transport hubs. Sensor data is processed in real time by a rules engine that detects anomalies, triggers alerts, and logs events for audits and compliance — before losses occur.

Built on open standards (MQTT, CoAP, HTTP), the platform supports cloud, on-premise, and edge deployments, allowing organizations to scale without vendor lock-in.

How it Works

A production-grade architecture that securely moves data from sensors to decisions — in real time and at scale.

3. Intelligence

4. Action
