About CONFIDENTIAL6G

CONFIDENTIAL6G is a European research initiative focused on integrating confidentiality and privacy guarantees into future 6G networks. The project researches secure computation at the edge, privacy-preserving protocols, and orchestration techniques to enable trustworthy mobile infrastructures.

Abstract Machines contributes in the areas of secure edge orchestration, lightweight gateways, and message-oriented middleware integration.

For details, see the project website and publications.

About

CONFIDENTIAL6G explores secure, private 6G connectivity, edge orchestration, and privacy-preserving telemetry for next-generation networks.

Goals

  • Design privacy-first 6G connectivity primitives
  • Prototype secure edge orchestration
  • Evaluate on realistic IoT workloads
