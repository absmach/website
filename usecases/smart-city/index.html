Smart Streetlights

Smarter Lighting for Safer, More Efficient Cities

View Live Demo Talk to an IoT Expert

Overview

Streetlighting is one of the most energy-intensive and operationally complex components of urban infrastructure. Traditional systems lack real-time visibility, resulting in delayed fault detection, inefficient energy usage, and high maintenance costs.

This use case illustrates how Abstract Machines enables cities and utilities to modernize streetlighting through centralized monitoring, adaptive control, and data-driven operations.

Magistrala supports production IoT systems used in distributed, long-lived environments such as smart city infrastructure. The platform enables secure device connectivity, real-time data processing, and centralized control across thousands of assets, operating reliably at both the cloud and edge. Its open architecture allows cities and integrators to evolve systems over time without dependence on proprietary vendors or hardware.

Key Challenges Addressed

  • Limited visibility into streetlight health and outages
  • High energy consumption due to static lighting schedules
  • Costly manual inspections and maintenance
  • Vendor lock-in limiting long-term flexibility

Solution Architecture

Streetlights equipped with smart controllers and sensors connect securely to Magistrala. The platform aggregates operational data such as power usage, lamp status, and fault conditions, enabling centralized monitoring and control.p>

Rules and automation workflows allow dynamic dimming based on time schedules, traffic conditions, or ambient light, while integrations expose data to city management systems and analytics platforms.

Key Capabilities

  • Real-time monitoring of streetlight health and status
  • Centralized control and dynamic dimming
  • Secure device management at scale
  • Event-driven alerts for faults and outages
  • Integration with existing city platforms and systems

Business Impact

  • Reduced energy consumption and operational costs
  • Faster fault detection and maintenance response
  • Improved public safety and service reliability
  • Long-term flexibility through open standards
