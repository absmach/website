Smart Streetlights

Intelligent urban lighting with real-time monitoring, dynamic dimming, and proactive maintenance.

View Live Demo

Overview

Urban lighting is a critical component of city infrastructure, directly impacting energy consumption, public safety, and operational costs. Traditional streetlight systems operate blindly, leading to wasted energy and delayed fault detection.

This smart streetlighting solution enables centralized control and monitoring of city-wide lighting assets. Connected streetlights continuously report their operational health, energy usage, and ambient conditions, allowing cities to optimize lighting schedules, reduce costs, and respond rapidly to failures.

Users & Roles

Urban Planning Architect

Oversees city-wide deployment, scaling, and infrastructure hierarchy.

  • Full read and update access
  • Manage devices, channels, and groups
  • Define city and zone hierarchies
City Operations Supervisor

Manages energy budgets, lighting schedules, and maintenance coordination.

  • Read-only operational visibility
  • Subscribe to telemetry channels
  • Manage team role assignments
Maintenance Electrician

Responds to outages and validates streetlight hardware in the field.

  • View device and channel data
  • Check signal strength (RSSI)
  • Send force ON/OFF test commands

System Structure

Groups & Zones

City infrastructure is organized hierarchically into logical zones such as districts and neighborhoods.

  • Main group: nairobi-smart-city
  • Subgroups: cbd-zone-a, upperhill-dist-1
Devices & Channels

Each streetlight controller reports telemetry through dedicated channels.

  • Devices: LIGHT-CTRL-CBD-001, LIGHT-CTRL-UPP-002
  • lighting-telemetry: Power, voltage, current
  • ambient-conditions: LUX measurements

Automation & Alarms

The rule engine continuously evaluates telemetry streams to detect failures and abnormal behavior in real time.

  • All telemetry is persisted automatically for auditing and analytics
  • Bulb failure detection when current drops while light state is ON
  • Critical outage alarms triggered instantly
  • Slack notifications sent to the maintenance team

Dashboards & Reports

Operational Dashboards

Real-time visibility into energy load, active lights, and system health across the city.

Control Widgets

Override switches and dimming sliders allow operators to adjust lighting behavior dynamically.

Geographic Monitoring

Map-based visualization shows streetlight status with color-coded markers for rapid issue identification.

Key Benefits

Energy Optimization

Dynamic dimming and scheduling reduce electricity costs.

Proactive Maintenance

Failures are detected instantly, minimizing downtime.

Improved Public Safety

Reliable lighting enhances security and urban livability.
